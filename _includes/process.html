04 / My approach

A clear process.
At every stage.

01 / RESEARCH

Understand the audience

Study the niche, viewer interests, relevant keywords and competing content.

02 / PLAN

Give the idea direction

Shape the topic, story, title and thumbnail into a coherent video concept.

Make the video

Prepare the script, voiceover and edit with attention to clarity and pacing.

04 / REVIEW

Learn from the results

Look at the analytics, identify weak points and choose the next improvement to test.
