A practical working process

Context first.
Then clear priorities.

Each step connects the creative decision to the channel's actual position.

01 / AUDIT

Understand the channel

Review the niche, audience, recent content, packaging and available analytics.

02 / PRIORITIZE

Choose what matters now

Separate urgent problems from lower-impact changes and define the working scope.

03 / BUILD

Prepare the improvement

Develop the research, plan, packaging or production support agreed for the project.

04 / REVIEW

Use results for the next step

Read the available data, document what happened and choose the next useful test.
