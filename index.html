Read the strategy & full results

The decisions

  • Selected the movie using keyword and trend research alongside story appeal and thumbnail potential.
  • Paired an English topic phrase with a Bengali curiosity-led title about a luxury-car heist.
  • Used luxury cars, masked figures and red–gold contrast in the thumbnail.
  • Opened directly with the story, without a separate channel introduction.

My role

Research, title and thumbnail, a self-written script with AI assistance, my own voiceover, editing in Adobe Premiere Pro, upload optimization and performance review.
